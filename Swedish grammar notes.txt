!! Sentence structure
Fundament  Verb1  (Subjekt)  Sats-Adverbial  Verb2  Objekt  Adverbial 

Fundament: represents start of a sentence, it can be a subject or a adverb representing time and place

Verb1 : In Swedish there is a rule V2 which means the first verb in a sentence will always come in second place. Verb1 can be a normal verb or a helper verb
Subjekt : This is optional and subject will be included in this position only if Fundament  is not a subject

Sats Adverbial : a special adverb(sentence adverb) which modifies or changes the meaning of an entire sentence
e.g 
inte (not)
kanske (maybe)
ofta (often)
alltid (always)
tyvärr (unfortunately)
aldrig (never)

Verb2 : A verb will be included for second time in the sentence if the first verb is a helper verb
e.g of helper verbs

Objekt represents answer to the questions what and who

!! Articles
Articles are words that indicate whether a noun is specific(definite) or general(indefinite)
en and ett are the indefinite(obestämd) articles
Definite (bestämd) form  is created by adding the article as a suffix
e.g. 
en bil (a car)
bilen (the car)
ett hus (a house)
huset ( the house)

When an adjective is used before a definite noun Swedish uses a double definite structure.
An additional standalone article(den det de) is placed before the adjective while the noun keeps its suffix
e.g.
den nya bilen ( the new car)
det gamma huset ( the old house)
de stora bilarna ( the new cars) -- for plural 

!! Noun
) en and ett genders
en and ett represent the genders of Swedish noun. There are no set rules to determine when they should be used. 74% of all nouns are en nouns and 26% are ett nouns. The best way to determine is to learn important ett nouns as they are few in number. Also statistically in Swedish the words ending with de te and ag are primarily ett words and words ending with a are almost always en words

!! PREPOSITION
Prepositions are short words (e.g., in, at, on, of, by, to) that connect nouns, pronouns, or phrases to other words within a sentence
)) Types of prepositions:

1. Place Prepositions (Lägesprepositioner)
Describe where something is (answers the question where?)

i -- in
e.g.
i) closed areas or areas with boundaries like country, city, village, province etc:
Jag bor i Europa (continent) --  I live in Europe
Jag bor i Sverige (country) 
Jag bor i Stockholm (city)

ii) Private, enclosed vehicles
i bilen ( in a car)

på -- on/at
e.g.
i) open spaces like island, street, address, square etc:
Jag bor på Gotland (island) -- I live on Gotland
Jag bor på Skogängsvägen 39A (address) -- I live at Skogängsvägen 39A

ii) places considered as points of activity or function
e.g. 
på jobbet (at work)
på skolan (at school)
på gymmet (at gym)

iii) Physical contact with a surface

iv) Public transport
på busen ( on the bus)
på tagen (on the train)
in, on, at, under, over, behind, beside, between, among

under -- under
Katten sitter under bordet (The cat is under the table)

över -- above
Lampan hängar över bordet (The lamp hangs above the table)

bakom -- behind

framför -- in front of

bredvid -- beside

mellan -- between (usually 2 things)
Huset ligger mellan två byggnader  (The house lies between two buildings)

bland  -- among (usually 3 or more things)
Han stod bland människorna (He stood among the people)

intill -- next to / adjacent to
Skolan ligger intill parken (The school lies next to the park)

nära -- near, close to
Jag bor nära centrum ( I live near the city centre)

i närhetten av  -- in the vicinity of (in the same area or neighbourhood but not directly next to each other)
Det finns en bra park i närheten av mitt jobb (There is a nice park near my work)

långt från -- far from
Vi bor långt från stan ( We live far from town)

mittemot -- across from / opposite to
Hon satt mittemot mig vid middagen (She sat opposite me at dinner)
Banken ligger mittemot posten (The bank is located opposite the post office)

till höger om -- to the right of 
Bilen står till höger om huset (The car is parked to the right of the house)

till vänster om  -- to the left of
Hon sitter till vänster om mig (She is sitting to the left of me)

Norr om -- north of

söder om -- south of
Staden ligger söder om sjön (The city is located south of the lake)

Öster om -- east of

Väster om -- west of


2. Direction Prepositions (Riktningsprepositioner)
Show movement towards or away from something

till -- to
Jag går till skolan (I walk to school)
Vi åker till Göteborg (We are going to Gothenburg)

från -- from
Jag kommer från Indien (I come from India)

mot -- toward
Bussen kör mot centrum (The bus drives towards the city centre)

genom -- through
Vi går genom skogen (We walk through the forest)

3. Time prepositions (Tidsprepositioner)
Used to indicate time

Categories of time prepositions:
i) Future time

a. In a future period
Use om when an event will happen after a period of time

om en timme (in an hour)
om två dagar (in two days)
om en vecka (in a week)
om tre månader (in three months)
om ett år (in a year)    
Tåget kommer om tio minuter (The train arrives in 10 minutes)

b. During a period
Use under

Jag kommer att resa till Indien under vintern(i will travel to India during winter)


c. Future month, year, season
Use i

i vinter (this winter)
i höst (this autumn)
i är (this year)

Jag ska åka till Sandhamn i september (I am going to Sandhamn in September)

d. Future day
Use på

Jag börjar mitt nya jobb på måndag (I start my new job on Monday)

d. Until / upto a future period
Use till to indicate endpoint of a future period

till fredag (until Friday)
till sommaren (until summer)

Use från ... till if we know the starting point and endpoint of a future period

från juni till augusti (from June to August)
från klockan två till fyra (from two to four)

Jag arbetar från måndag till fredag (I work from Monday to Friday)

ii) Past time
a. Ago
Use för + amount of time + sedan to represent how long ago something happened

Jag flyttade till Sverige för två år sedan ( I moved to Sweden two years ago )
Hon ringde mig för en timme sedan ( She called me an hour ago )

b. In a past period
Use i when talking about a specific period in the past, especially months, years, seasons and historical periods

I går (yesterday)
i förrgår (day before yesterday)
i måndags ( last Monday)
i somras ( last summer )
i januari ( in January)

c. During a past period
Use under(during) to represent something that happened throughout or a somepoint within a past period

under sommaren (during the summer)
under semestern (during the vacation)
under helgen (during the weeekend)
under 1990-talet (during the 1990s)

Jag bodde i Stockholm under sommaren ( I lived in Stockholm during the summer )


iii) Present perfect
Use sedan(since/for) to represent an event which started in the past and continues to the present

Jag har bott här sedan 2025 ( I have lived here since 2025 )
Hon har arbetat här sedan januari ( She has worked here since January )

iv) Habitual or repeating actions
Use på

Jag åker till Djurgården på söndagar ( I go to Djurgården on Sundays )
Jag går till jobbet på vardagar ( i go to work on weekdays )
Jag går till gymmet på helgen ( i go to gym on weekend )
Jag spelar på helgerna ( I play on weekends)

v) Holidays
Use på
Vad gjorde du på midsommar? ( What did you do on Midsummer? )

vi) Negative duration ( the amount of time that has passed without doing something)
Use på 

Jag har inte tränat på en vecka ( I haven't worked out for a week )
Jag har inte ätit pizza på en månad ( i have not eaten pizza in a month )

vii) Time taken to complete a task
Use på to  show how long it takes to finish an action. This is used for past, present, and future actions. 

Jag kan laga bilen på två dagar ( I can fix the car in two days )
Han sprang milen på 45 minuter ( He ran the 10 kilometers in 45 minutes )

viii) Specific time
Use klockan to represent a specific time

Vi ses klockan 20.00 ( We will meet at 8:00 pm)

ix) Around a time or special event (including holiday)
Use vid
vid 8-tiden (around 8.00 clock)
Vid midsommar (Around Midsummer)
Vid jul  (Around Christmas)
Vid 18 års ålder (At 18 years of age)

Vi ska träffas vid sjutiden ( We will meet around seven )
Vi åker till Jämtland vid midsommar ( We are going to Jämtland around Midsummer )


x) Precise Moments or Turning Points ("At")
Use vid to point to a specific, sharp moment in time when an action or state changes

Tåget anländer vid midnatt ( The train arrives at midnight )

xi) How fast?
Use på to answer how fast something is completed

Jag kan avsluta arbetet på en timme ( i can finish the work in 1 hour )
Hon springer 1 km på fem minuter ( She runs 1 km in five minutes )
